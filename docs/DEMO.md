# Devpost Demo Script

## Scenario

A senior receives:

> Congratulations! You have won ৳50,000. Verify immediately. Send your OTP to claim.

## Demo

1. Select the message.
2. Click SafeScroll.
3. Show `Analyzing...`.
4. Reveal `🔴 SCAM — HIGH RISK`.
5. Reveal evidence one by one: OTP request, urgency, prize bait.
6. Read the warning aloud.
7. Ask: “What should I do?”
8. Show simple actions: do not share OTP, do not click, verify through an official channel.

The key story is human decision support, not the extension technology itself.
